Grouping

Each container gets one tab group. Because a tab group can’t span windows, a tab may be moved to whichever window already holds its container’s group.

Puts tabs opened outside any Multi-Account Container into a “No Container” group.

When off, groups are created once and then left alone for you to rename.

Where a tab joins its group

Applies as tabs are added; existing tabs aren’t reordered.

A blank new tab (Ctrl+T, the “+” button) inherits the container of the tab you were on. Links already open in their own tab’s container.

Open sites in a container

When you open a matching site, the tab is reopened in the chosen container (and therefore lands in that container’s group). Matching an already-correct container is left alone; any other container is moved.
